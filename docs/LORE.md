# Lore research notes

These are the notes used to build *Amber and Chaos*. Facts were gathered from the
web (see **Sources**) rather than from memory alone. Where the game takes
liberties for the sake of play, that is listed under **Game inventions** at the end.

## The two true worlds

- **Amber** is the one true world of Order. All other worlds are its *Shadows*,
  spread on a continuum between Amber and its opposite, the **Courts of Chaos**,
  which stand at the very edge of an **Abyss**.
- Shadows arise from the tension between the forces of Amber and of Chaos. Physics
  varies from Shadow to Shadow, and time runs at different rates. Gunpowder does
  not ignite in Amber, which is why its princes carry swords.
- **The Pattern** is inscribed on the floor of a cavern deep in **Mount Kolvir**,
  beneath Castle Amber. Walking it is like wading in lead boots, with three veils
  (the First, the Second and the Final). Stopping or straying means a terrible
  death. Whoever reaches the centre gains the power to walk in Shadow.
- **The Primal Pattern** was made by **Dworkin Barimen**, a lord of Chaos and
  Oberon's father, using the Jewel. When Brand spilled the blood of Random's son
  Martin on it, the stain took the shape of the corrupted **Vale of Garnath**, and
  that stain was the source of the **Black Road**. Its guardian was a purple
  griffin named **Wixer**.
- **Broken Patterns** are imperfect copies of the true Pattern. Sorcerers who walk
  them draw power directly from the break, which is "similar to drawing their
  power from Chaos".
- **The Logrus** is the Pattern's three-dimensional opposite. It is an ever-changing
  tangle of tentacles. Navigating it grants passage through Shadow and the power to
  *pull* things out of Shadow. Its keeper is **Suhuy**.
- **The Unicorn** is the emblem of Amber, and it never speaks. **The Serpent of
  Chaos** is its counterpart. The Jewel of Judgment was the Serpent's left eye.

## Artifacts

- **The Jewel of Judgment** is a large red gem worn as a pendant. At first it is a
  weather-control device; Eric's storms with it wiped out Corwin and Bleys's
  assault on Amber. It **draws on its bearer's life force**. Corwin used it to
  inscribe his own Pattern at the edge of Chaos.
- **Trumps** are hand-painted, tarot-like cards. Concentrating on one opens a
  psychic link to the person or place shown, which allows talk, travel, and even
  attack. Dworkin taught their making. Brand later became a *living Trump* who
  could go anywhere in Shadow by will.
- **Grayswandir** is Corwin's sword. It was forged on the stairway to
  Tir-na Nog'th and has special properties there.
- **Jeweler's rouge** from the Shadow **Avalon** is the only powder that fires in
  Amber. Corwin bought weapons from Earth with diamonds gathered in a Shadow where
  "South Africa was never mined", where the stones lie loose on the sand. His
  riflemen were the same Shadow-creatures he had led in his first assault, people
  who regarded him as a god.
- **The Ghostwheel** is Merlin's Trump- and Pattern-based computer.
- **Spikards** are rings or swords tied to power-caches across Shadow.

## People of Amber (with their heraldic colours)

| Name | Facts used |
|---|---|
| Oberon | King of Amber, son of Dworkin. His disappearance begins the war of succession. He walked among his children disguised as Ganelon. |
| Corwin | Silver and black. Swordsman, narrator, wielder of Grayswandir. He regenerates (even his blinded eyes regrew). He purged part of the Black Road by holding the image of the Pattern in his mind. His curse on Eric opened the way for the Black Road. His horse was **Drum**. |
| Benedict | Orange, yellow and brown. Master of Arms of Amber, an "unmatched master of all martial weapons", and Protector of Avalon. He lost an arm fighting the **Hellmaids** of Chaos, and his armies were so vast that their columns seemed endless. |
| Eric | Black, red and silver. The greatest swordsman after Benedict. He held Amber with the Jewel's storms and died fighting the creatures of the Black Road, leaving Corwin the Jewel. |
| Gérard | Blue and grey. "The strongest man in the multiverse". He co-commanded Amber's navy and guarded Amber during the final war. |
| Julian | White and black. Cold hunter and lord of the **Forest of Arden**. His hellhounds can rip flesh and metal, and he killed the manticore that attacked Corwin. His horse is Morgenstern. |
| Caine | Black and green. Naval commander and archer. He faked his own death, and then killed Brand with silver-tipped arrows at the Courts of Chaos. |
| Random | Orange, red and brown. Drummer and glider pilot. The Unicorn chose him to be king after Patternfall. |
| Fiona | Sorceress and Dworkin's best student. She bound Brand psychically at the edge of the Abyss. |
| Bleys | Red and orange. Charming swordsman who joined Corwin's assault on Amber and fell from Kolvir. |
| Brand | Green. Mad sorcerer who bargained with Chaos. He was pyrokinetic, a *living Trump*, and was imprisoned in a tower guarded by a glass dragon. |
| Deirdre | Silver and black. Fought in black plate with an axe. She fell into the Abyss with Brand. |
| Llewella | Lives in **Rebma**, the sunken mirror of Amber beneath the sea, ruled by Queen Moire. |
| Merlin | Purple and grey. Son of Corwin and Dara. Walked both the Pattern and the Logrus. |

## People and creatures of Chaos

- **Dara** is Benedict's great-granddaughter through the Hellmaid **Lintra**, and
  Merlin's mother. She was trained in the sword by **Borel**. She walked the
  Pattern of Amber in the middle of a battle and changed into a creature of Chaos.
- **Borel** is Duke and Master of Arms of **House Hendrake**. **Gilva** is a
  warrior-maiden of the same house.
- **House Sawall** includes Gramble Sawall (Dara's husband), **Mandor** (a
  Machiavellian manipulator whose name is an anagram of "Random"), **Jurt**
  (who hates Merlin) and Despil. **Swayvill** is King of Chaos.
- Creatures of the Black Road and the Courts include:
  - the **goat-faced demon** who led the creatures of the Circle in Lorraine, and
    whose followers called Corwin "opener";
  - **manticores**, **wyvern-riders** and **Hellmaids**;
  - spined humanoids with bloodshot eyes, who hunted Random across Shadow;
  - the bodiless, possessing **ty'iga**;
  - Courts **crossbowmen** and pale riders.
- **The Black Road** runs from the Courts of Chaos to the foot of Kolvir. Its
  grass is tentacular and drains the life from living things.
- **Ygg** is a talking tree planted by Oberon to mark the border between Order and
  Chaos. **Hugi** is a fatalist talking raven.

## Places

Amber, Castle Amber, Kolvir, Arden, Rebma, Tir-na Nog'th, Avalon, Lorraine
(with the Circle), the Vale of Garnath, the Grove of the Unicorn, Cabra (and its
lighthouse, kept by Jopin), Begma, Texorami, the Courts of Chaos, the Abyss,
Thelbane, and the Keep of Four Worlds.

## Game inventions

To make a playable RTS, the game:

- invents rank-and-file unit names, such as the Stewards of Amber, the Thralls of
  Chaos and the Tidecallers of Rebma;
- turns the second resource into **Order** (for Amber) and **Flux** (for Chaos),
  drawn from Broken Pattern sites;
- makes Chaos buildings grow only on the Black Road;
- gives the heroes ability kits that dramatise their canon traits. Examples are
  Corwin's *Image of the Pattern*, Fiona's *Psychic Binding*, Caine's
  *Silver Arrow*, Brand's *Living Trump* and the Ty'iga's *Possession*.

The campaign retells or extrapolates book events, and is not a transcript of them.
